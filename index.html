Selected projects

PCM blood warmer enclosure

Individual project. CAD modelling in Fusion 360.

The housing for a phase-change-material (PCM) based blood warmer concept. The enclosure model explores a rectangular housing with a hinged lid and an internal compartment layout. The model communicates the overall form, the opening arrangement, and the relationship between the enclosure body and lid.

Design elements

  • Main enclosure body and lid geometry
  • Hinged opening arrangement
  • Internal layout and component clearances represented in the model

Roller mechanism assembly

Individual project. Mechanical assembly modelling in Autodesk Inventor.

A mechanical roller mechanism modelled as an assembly. It brings together a roller, shaft, supporting brackets, bushings, base plate and standard fasteners. The assembled model, shown at the top of this page, presents the component arrangement. The exploded drawing shows how the parts relate during assembly.

Assembly components

  • Roller and shaft
  • Supporting brackets and base plate
  • Bushings and standard fasteners

FDM-printed spur gears

Group project. I handled the FDM 3D printing.

Two spur gears designed and modelled by our group, then produced with FDM 3D printing, which I handled. The physical parts are a tangible outcome of the CAD work and show a basic digital-to-physical prototyping workflow.

Process

  1. CAD modelling. The group created the gear geometry in a 3D CAD workflow.
  2. Additive manufacturing. I produced the physical parts with FDM 3D printing.
  3. Physical outcome. Reviewed the printed components as tangible prototypes.

What this project demonstrates

Hands-on FDM additive manufacturing, working from a shared CAD model, and the translation of a digital model into a physical part.
